# Agilent / Keysight 34922A — multiplexer switching contract v1

This module is composed through the one 34980A factory/session in `34980A.md`. Primary evidence: `research/34922A-user.pdf`, shared 34921A–34925A guide, printed/PDF 21–23 for SCPI switching and 34–35 for **34922A** topology. Neighboring 34921A/34923A/34924A pages are not interchangeable evidence.

## Admitted subset

Bind positive logical switch channels to mainframe slots 1–8 and local channels 1–70. The module has two banks of **35 latching, two-wire channels**: bank 1 is 1–35, bank 2 is 36–70. Analog bus channels 911–914 and 921–924 exist but are not admitted in this initial subset. Their hardware interlocks are not bypassed or normalized.

Support direct switching only. Require no active scan and no other controller. Do not offer DMM acquisition, scan, four-wire measurement, analog-bus routing or OpenAll. Internal four-wire DMM measurements automatically pair channel n with n+35; this does not occur for the admitted direct two-wire ROUTe operations. Starting DMM scans has broader cross-slot analog-bus effects and remains deferred.

In non-scanning mode, the armature module permits multiple channels closed in a bank. **CloseChannel is not exclusive, does not open another path and can connect multiple external signals to a common.** The application must commission wiring and control which sources may be connected. Do not copy the FET module's one-channel-per-bank or break-before-make behavior. Channel relays retain state with power off; analog bus relays open. Resource close neither resets nor opens channels.

## Exact operations

Derive `a = 1000*slot + local`:

- Switch.CloseChannel: write `:ROUT:CLOS (@a)`.
- Switch.OpenChannel: write `:ROUT:OPEN (@a)`.
- Switch.ChannelIsClosed: query `:ROUT:CLOS? (@a)`, accepting exactly `0` or `1`.

These messages match the common parent switching API. There is no invented matrix endpoint, automatic paired channel or list/range command. A Boolean reply reports the device's commanded path state, not measured continuity or electrical protection. Other reply tokens, whitespace, CSV and blocks are Parse.

Validate all mapping/arguments before IO. Errors/cancellation quarantine the parent and affect every module view. No automatic route cleanup, bus reset, retry or physical safety action occurs. Ownership and sync/native/worker behavior follow `34980A.md`.

## Independent acceptance

Cover direct paths 1,35,36,70 and slots 1/8; same-bank sequential closures must issue both close writes without implicit open; both state values; malformed states; invalid local0/71, all analog-bus locals, duplicate/type conflicts and sparse mapping. Test ownership, pending timeout/cancellation and shared-parent quarantine. State explicitly that scalar acquisition and hardware evidence remain absent.
