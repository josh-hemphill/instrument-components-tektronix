Connectors enforce finite deadlines and256-byte allocation at the transport boundary, remove LF/EOI framing only and reject binary wrappers before stripping headers. Scope calls leave HEAD OFF/VERB ON; ESR reads consume history. Fresh scalar calls replace acquisition with SAMPLE/SEQUENCE and end stopped. Cancellation can leave an acquisition running; external resynchronization/recommission/reopen is required, and close sends no inferred cleanup. Probe/electrical settings and physical input protection remain application responsibilities.
